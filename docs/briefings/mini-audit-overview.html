OSINT Recon Toolkit · Project 6.1Executive methodology brief

Authorized external
reconnaissance mini-audit

A bounded review of existing third-party records for asset context, coverage decisions and analyst follow-up.

Methodology overview. Genuine live acceptance, commercial readiness and real client validation remain unverified. Offline examples are synthetic.

Objective

Review registration context, indexed service metadata and reputation signals; separate observations, gaps and items requiring authorized verification.

Scope and authority

One approved domain or public IP per assessment. Record owner approval, exact target, sources, time window, recipients and retention. Related assets do not expand scope.

Available intelligence sources

SourceSelected evidence and boundary
RDAPRegistration events/status/nameservers or IP context through curated HTTPS registries. RDAP is implemented; legacy port-43 WHOIS is not.
ShodanExisting host or domain/DNS metadata and indexed ports. Domain coverage is page 1; subscription access may be required.
VirusTotal v3Existing domain/IP reputation and analysis statistics. No uploads or rescans. Classifications do not establish compromise.

Controlled workflow and evidence collection

  1. Approve: record scope; check terms, entitlements and quotas; secure server-side keys.
  2. Collect: start a fresh approved assessment. Retain source, target, UTC attempt/retrieval time, provider dates and observed/unavailable/error state.
  3. Review: separate in-scope observations, interpretation, uncertainty and recommendations.
  4. Deliver and retain: reconcile exports, document limits, obtain sign-off and apply approved access, transfer and retention controls.

Outputs and deliverables

Approval record and run log; reviewed HTML report with A4 styling; JSON and CSV evidence; coverage notes, recommendations and analyst/reviewer sign-off checklist.

Licensing and readiness gates

VirusTotal public terms restrict commercial use; obtain an appropriate commercial agreement/key where required. A software acknowledgement grants no license. Confirm Shodan plan access, quotas, privacy controls and authorized live acceptance.

Limits to communicate

No scanning, exploitation, direct target contact or automatic scope expansion. Records may be incomplete or stale; missing evidence never proves safety. This local, single-operator tool supplies no exhaustive inventory, encrypted/signed custody or public multi-user deployment.

References: workbook, scope form, checklist and compliance matrix. Demo needs no target or keys.
